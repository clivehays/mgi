The Manager Gap self-check

Eight questions, about ninety seconds, and the state your team is most likely in.

This is the short scorecard taken from the full assessment. It keeps the instinct check the assessment opens with, three of its fifteen evidence questions, and the four that place your team. For 44 of the first 50 managers who answered all twenty, these eight named the same state. Where the two differed, the short version pointed at the harder reading every time, never the easier one.

  1. Cruise Output holding, and the behaviours of a healthy team still reach you.
  2. Drift Output holding while the conditions underneath it slide. Nothing in the numbers says anything is wrong.
  3. Headwinds Output under pressure, with a real force outside the team acting on it.
  4. Stall Output has stopped moving, and the signals went quiet long before it did.

Nothing is sent to you. Your scorecard appears on this page the moment you finish.

Before you start: answer from memory of actual events. There are no wrong answers. But a guessed answer makes the result meaningless. “Can’t recall” is a valid and useful answer.

This is for anyone who leads a team directly: managers, heads of department, directors, and founders with people reporting to them. Company size does not matter. It asks about one team, the one you lead now.

No name, no email, no company. Your answers are stored under a random number so the short version can be checked against the full one, and nothing individual is ever published.
