The Manager Gap Index

Every manager carries a picture of their team. How much of yours is current?

Your read on your team is built from two sources: things you have actually seen recently, and assumptions carried forward from the last time you looked. Teams change faster than snapshots. This diagnostic measures how much of your picture is running on fresh signal, and how much is running on memory.

Twelve questions. About three minutes. Every question is about your own recent experience, so only you can answer it, and only honest answers produce a meaningful result.

Before you start: answer from memory of actual events. There are no wrong answers. But a guessed answer makes the result meaningless. “Can’t recall” is a valid and useful answer.

Your signal reading

What you could detect

Teams move through four states. Cruise: stable and healthy. Drift: output still fine, but the things that produce it are slipping. Headwinds: output falling while the team itself is healthy; the cause is outside. Stall: the decline has reached the output. Stall is the only state every manager detects without trying, because by then it has already cost you.
