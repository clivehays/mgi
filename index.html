What the first forty found

Forty managers have completed a reading, as at 15 October 2026.

  • Instinct and reading disagreed for 25 of 40, and the lean is towards worry.
  • Challenge is the first thing to go quiet: 20 of 40 had not seen open disagreement in over a month. Nine could not recall it at all.
  • Output told them nothing: output improved or held for 31 of 40. Twelve of those 31 were in Drift.
The four states
  1. Cruise Output holding, and the behaviours of a healthy team still reach you.
  2. Drift Output holding while the conditions underneath it slide. Nothing in the numbers says anything is wrong.
  3. Headwinds Output under pressure, with a real force outside the team acting on it.
  4. Stall Output has stopped moving, and the signals went quiet long before it did.
What you get

What you get

A reading of about a page. It names the state your team is most likely in, and how much of the team’s week actually reaches you.

It scores five areas on what has reached you recently, then picks the one worth your attention now and gives you something to do about it this week. There is no score out of ten and no league table.

Before you start

Answer from memory of actual events. There are no wrong answers. But a guessed answer makes the result meaningless. “Can’t recall” is a valid and useful answer.

For anyone who leads a team directly. It asks about one team, the one you lead now.

Your answers are private. Nothing individual is ever published.

What happens to your answers

What happens to your answers

Your answers are stored so the cohort can be studied as a whole. Nothing individual is ever published. The research that comes out of this, including the Index and the book, reports patterns across everyone who takes part, never one person’s answers.

An email address is optional. Give one and your report is sent to you, and Clive may write to you once about your result. That is the only contact. Leave it blank and your report opens on a private link at the end instead, and nothing is sent to you at all. Nothing here is sold or passed to anyone else, and you can have your answers deleted at any time by writing to contact@cloverera.com.

Nothing from LinkedIn loads unless you accept the cookie banner. That tag is how a visit from an advert is counted, and you can change the choice at any time from Cookie settings in the footer. The page also records how far each visit gets through the questions, under a random number that is discarded when you close the tab and is never attached to your answers, your name or your email. That is used for one thing: seeing where the questions lose people.
